      Words used here
      Wh, kWh
      Watt-hours: energy. A 100 W appliance running for 5 hours uses 500 Wh. 1,000 Wh is one kilowatt-hour (kWh).
      Ah
      Amp-hours: how much current a battery can supply for how long. Multiply by the battery's voltage to get Wh: 200 Ah at 12 V is 2,400 Wh.
      LiFePO4
      Lithium iron phosphate, a common lithium battery chemistry for off-grid use. Lead-acid is the older, heavier alternative.
      Safe floor
      The lowest charge the model lets the battery reach before calling it too deep. Draining lead-acid batteries deeply shortens their life.
      Peak sun hours
      The day's sunshine expressed as hours of full-strength sun. 4.5 means a day delivers as much energy as 4.5 hours at full strength.
      Derate
      The share of a panel's label rating that you really get after heat, dust, wiring and charger losses.
      Inverter
      The box that turns the battery's DC into AC for ordinary appliances. It wastes some energy and has a maximum power.
